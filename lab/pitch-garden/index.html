For grown-ups: how it works

Pitch Garden listens for the fundamental frequency of your child's voice (a standard autocorrelation pitch-detector, running entirely in the browser) and maps it onto a five-note scale. Each round spells one short word: every new letter sits one note higher than the last, so spelling the word out loud is also singing a short rising tune — a gentle bridge between phonics and ear training. A child can sing a hum, a long "ahh", or the letter sound itself; exact pitch accuracy isn't required, just getting close and holding it for under a second. The arrow keys (or the on-screen ▲▼ buttons) control the same game without any singing at all, for a quieter turn or a shared classroom device. No audio is ever recorded, stored or sent anywhere — the microphone, if you allow it, is read live and only in this tab.
