For grown-ups: how it works

Word Safari pairs a German word with its Slovenian translation — not with a picture — so the match itself is the lesson: to find the pair, a child has to recognise (or guess, then remember) that two whole words mean the same thing. Each level starts on a Study screen that shows every pair together with its picture and a speaker button, then switches to a shuffled matching grid to test recall.

Pronunciation uses your browser's built-in text-to-speech (German and Slovenian voices, where installed) — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. If your device has no Slovenian voice installed, those words will simply stay silent; the game still works fully by sight.
