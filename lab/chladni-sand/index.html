Presets

Nodal lines are the places where the plate does not move. Grains get kicked around everywhere else, so they wander until they happen to land on a still line, and stay there. Higher n and m mean more bends in the plate, a higher pitch (the Hz shown is a made-up scale) and finer lines.

Things to try

  1. Predict first: before moving a slider, guess whether the pattern gets more lines or fewer. Then do it.
  2. Turn on sound and raise n and m: the pitch rises as the pattern gets finer.
  3. Set mix to +1, then −1. They are mirror families; 0 shows a plain grid of squares.
  4. Press sweep and watch the sand rearrange as the pitch glides between patterns.
  5. Drag over the plate to pour fresh sand where you like.
