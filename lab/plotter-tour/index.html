How it works

This is the travelling salesman problem with an open end: visit every dot once, as short as possible. Finding the perfect answer is very hard, so plotter software settles for good answers fast. Nearest neighbour is greedy: from the current dot, go to the closest unvisited one. It starts well and paints itself into corners, leaving a few long hops at the end (shown in orange). 2-opt looks for two travel lines that cross and fixes them by reversing the stretch of the tour between them; every fix makes the tour strictly shorter, and it stops when no crossings are left.

Time estimate: the pen flies at 60 mm/s and spends 0.15 s on every dot. Real plotters differ, but the ratio is the point. The SVG holds the dots in tour order, so a plotter program such as Inkscape's AxiDraw extension will draw them in that sequence.
