Besedopad

Two-letter capsules fall like in Tetris or Dr. Mario. Move and rotate them to spell a real Slovenian word — 3 letters or more — across a row or down a column. The word clears and scores; longer words are worth disproportionately more, and letters falling into place afterwards can chain into more words.

For grown-ups: how it works

Every letter-pair is weighted by how often that letter actually shows up across the word list, so playable letters come up often. After a capsule locks, every row and column is scanned for the longest non-overlapping stretches that spell a real word; a match in a row and a match in a column can cross the same tile and both score, crossword-style. Scoring is roughly quadratic in word length (points = length × (length − 1) × 10), and gravity cascades after a clear can chain into further words for a growing multiplier — so hunting for one long word beats clearing several short ones.

The word list (about 83,500 single Slovenian words, 3–10 letters) was extracted from the borismarn/english-slovenian-dictionary project's 188,000-entry English→Slovenian dataset — filtering out phrases, proper nouns and a few untranslated English leaks in the source data. The short gloss shown next to each cleared word is its original English dictionary entry, so the interface stays EN/DE/SL but the glosses themselves are English.
