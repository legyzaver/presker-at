Slikovar

Tap any picture and hear its name spoken aloud in Slovenian — by a real offline neural voice, not the browser's own. Animals, food and home, A to Ž.

For grown-ups: how it works

Every word here is pre-recorded, not read live: each clip was generated once, offline, with Piper — a small open-source neural text-to-speech engine — using its Slovenian voice ("sl_SI-artur-medium"). The finished page just plays back those 24 audio files; nothing is synthesized in the browser and nothing is sent anywhere when a child taps a picture. That also means it sounds the same for every visitor, unlike the browser speech synthesis used elsewhere in this lab, which depends on whatever voices happen to be installed on each device.
