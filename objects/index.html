Insta360 One RS 1-Inch → DJI Battery Adapter

Insta360 stopped making the battery for the One RS 1-Inch 360 (Leica). This adapter runs the camera off a DJI Osmo Pocket 3 battery over USB-C — so a stranded camera works again.

Fisher Price Record Plates

3D-printable discs for the 1971 Fisher Price toy record player — raised bumps that pluck a metal comb like a music box. Includes FP Disk Burner, a browser tool to turn any melody into a printable disc, plus the build log and first print.
