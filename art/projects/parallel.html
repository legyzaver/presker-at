Description

In collaboration with Luiza Margan. A single solid block, its faces covered in a marbled, crystalline pink-and-white surface, sits alone in a dark corner — minimal in form, dense in material. Only two documentation images survive (titled "alt" and "fock" in the original files), suggesting a quieter, more contained piece than the larger room installations elsewhere on this site.

Concept

In collaboration with Luiza Margan. The title refers to the parallel practices of two artists shown in proximity: Miha Presker's photographic series Focus Free (2005–08) and Luiza Margan's Altered Relations (2008). The pairing examines how different working methods — one optical and accumulative, one material and sculptural — produce related spatial and perceptual questions without converging into a single statement.
