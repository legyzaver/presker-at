Title
Homo Diaphoricus
Year
2004
Medium
Animation, black-and-white

Description

A stylized black-and-white animation exploring environmental isolation, tribal geographies, and technological intersection. An abstract figure navigates a sequence of landscapes — from primitive hut clusters to mechanized aircraft formations — ending in a reflection on the boundary between species and category.
