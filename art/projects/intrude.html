Title
Intrude
Medium
Night-time projection on building facade
Dimensions
[variable]

Description

Dozens of small human silhouettes — falling, climbing, leaping — are projected across the entire facade of a residential building at night, scattered between windows and over a balustrade as if a crowd of intruders were caught mid-motion, scaling the building's surface.

Concept

The title suggests trespass or invasion staged at architectural scale: bodies that shouldn't be on a building's exterior, multiplied and frozen across it. The piece turns a private facade into a site of mass, ambiguous intrusion — uncanny rather than threatening, given the small scale and stilled, suspended poses of the figures.
